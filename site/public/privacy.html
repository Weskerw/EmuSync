EmuSync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

EmuSync is a free, open-source desktop application that keeps your emulator save files in sync between your computers, through your own Google Drive. This policy explains what it does with your data.

Your save files never pass through us. They travel directly between your computer and your own Google Drive. An EmuSync account — hosted on Google Firebase — holds only your settings and a list of file names and checksums, so that your computers can agree on what is already in sync.

What the app accesses

  • The folders you choose. EmuSync reads and writes only the local save folders you add, plus its own settings folder.
  • Your Google Drive, through the drive.file scope. This scope restricts the app to files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see, read, or modify anything else in your Drive.
  • Sign-in. Either Google — handled by Google's own consent screen, so the app receives a token and never your password — or an email address and a password, handled by Firebase Authentication. Passwords are never stored by EmuSync in any readable form.

What is stored, and where

  • Your saves: in your Google Drive. Uploaded to a folder named EmuSync, with one subfolder per emulator, under your own account and storage quota. They are stored nowhere else.
  • Your account and settings: in Firebase. The project is administered by the developer and hosted by Google. It contains your email address and account identifier, the list of emulators you sync with their labels, the names you give your devices and the local folders configured on each, and an index of the synced files: relative path, size, modification date and MD5 checksum. It also keeps a history of your syncs — which device did what, when, and to which files — for one year, after which it is deleted automatically. No file content is ever uploaded there.
  • On your computer. The authorization tokens (the Firebase one encrypted for your Windows user), a copy of the settings and the record of the last sync, under %APPDATA%\EmuSync.

Security rules restrict each account's data so that it can only be read or written by that account. The developer, as administrator of the Firebase project, has technical access to the stored data described above, and does not use it for any purpose other than operating the sync.

What is collected

Beyond what is listed above, nothing. EmuSync contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Firebase records the usual technical information needed to operate an authentication service, such as sign-in timestamps.

Data sharing

User data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the sync feature described on this site, and is never transferred to anyone. EmuSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access, and deleting your data

  • Sign out from within the app (Settings → Sign out of EmuSync), which deletes the tokens stored on that computer.
  • Disconnect Drive only, keeping your account: Settings → Change Google Drive account.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the EmuSync folder in your Google Drive to remove the uploaded saves, and uninstall by deleting EmuSync.exe and the %APPDATA%\EmuSync folder.
  • To have your EmuSync account and everything stored with it erased, open an issue at github.com/Weskerw/EmuSync/issues from the address you signed up with. Your saves on Drive are yours and are not touched by this.

Children

EmuSync is not directed at children under 13, who should not create an account. No information is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact
