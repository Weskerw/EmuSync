EmuSync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

EmuSync is a free, open-source desktop application that copies your emulator save files between your computer and your own Google Drive. This policy explains what it does with your data. The short version: it does not collect any.

There is no EmuSync server. Your files travel directly between your computer and Google Drive, and the developer has no access to them, to your Google account, or to any information about your use of the app.

What the app accesses

  • The folders you choose. EmuSync reads and writes only the local folders you add as sync profiles.
  • Your Google Drive, through the drive.file scope. This scope restricts the app to files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see, read, or modify anything else in your Drive.
  • Google account sign-in. Handled by Google's own consent screen. The app receives an access token, never your password.

What is stored, and where

  • Your save files are uploaded to a folder named EmuSync in your own Google Drive, under your own account and storage quota.
  • The authorization token and the app settings (the list of folders and options) are stored locally on your computer, under %APPDATA%\EmuSync. They never leave your machine.
  • Nothing is stored on any server operated by the developer, because there is none.

What is collected

Nothing. EmuSync contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. No personal data is collected, transmitted to the developer, sold, or shared.

Data sharing

User data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the sync feature described on this site, and is never transferred to anyone. EmuSync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • Sign out from within the app (Settings → Change Google account), which deletes the stored token.
  • Revoke the app entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the EmuSync folder in your Google Drive to remove the uploaded saves, and uninstall by deleting EmuSync.exe and the %APPDATA%\EmuSync folder.

Children

EmuSync is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them — or, for that matter, from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/Weskerw/EmuSync/issues.
