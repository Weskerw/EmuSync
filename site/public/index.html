Your emulator saves,
on every PC

EmuSync keeps the save files and memory cards of your emulators in sync through your own Google Drive. Stop a run on the desktop, pick it up on the laptop.

Download for Windows View source

Free and open source · Single .exe, no installer · Windows 10/11

What it does

Point EmuSync at the save folder of each emulator you use. From then on it looks after itself.

One profile per emulator

PCSX2, Dolphin, RetroArch, anything else — each folder syncs to its own folder on your Drive, subfolders included.

Syncs the moment you save

EmuSync watches the folders and uploads shortly after the emulator finishes writing. No polling, no measurable CPU or disk cost.

Picks up other PCs

A periodic check pulls down saves that another machine uploaded, so the newest progress is always the one waiting for you.

Never overwrites blindly

Files are compared by content hash first. If they differ, the more recently modified one wins — in either direction.

Out of your way

Optionally starts with Windows, hidden in the system tray, with sync and exit one click away.

Sign in once

Connect your Google account on first launch. The token is stored on your PC and you are not asked again.

How it works

Three minutes of setup, then you can forget about it.

  1. Download and run EmuSync. It is one executable — nothing to install.
  2. Sign in with Google. Your browser opens once to grant access to the app's own folder on your Drive.
  3. Add your save folders. Give each one a name, and EmuSync mirrors it to EmuSync/<name> on Drive from then on.

Your saves stay yours

  • Files go straight from your PC to your own Google Drive. There is no EmuSync server in the middle, and no account to create.
  • EmuSync asks for the drive.file permission only, which limits it to the files it creates itself. The rest of your Drive stays invisible to it.
  • Nothing is collected, tracked, or sent anywhere else. The source code is public, so you can check.
